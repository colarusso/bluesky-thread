Unroll and Explore Bluesky Threads

Drag Thread to your toolbar to unroll & explore Bluesky threads right from the first post. That is, if you place the bookmarklet in your browser's toolbar, you can click it while in a thread to unroll or explore the thread. The tool will remember your tab preference from its last use, unless overwritten in the URL.
